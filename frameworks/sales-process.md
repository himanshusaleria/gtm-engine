## Preview

The Sales Process framework defines seven sequential phases that a deal moves through from initial research to close. Each phase has specific objectives, activities, and exit criteria. It provides a repeatable checklist so you know exactly what needs to happen at each stage — and can diagnose where deals stall. Think of it as the operational playbook that sits alongside your pipeline scorecard.

## What You Can Use Now

- **[Pipeline Scorecard](pipeline-scorecard.md)** — Track deal health and commitment across your pipeline. While it doesn't prescribe a process, it tells you which deals need attention.
- **[Customer Profile](customer-profile.md)** — Covers the research and qualification phases of any sales process. Start by knowing who you're selling to.
- **[Discovery Questions](discovery-questions.md)** — Essential for the discovery and qualification phases of the sales process.
