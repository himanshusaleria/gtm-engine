## Preview

The Commitment Matrix is a detailed scoring model that evaluates prospect commitment across four factors — Time, Energy, Money, and Qualitative signals — in two domains: what prospects say (Words) and what they actually do (Actions). It provides a granular, evidence-based view of deal health that goes beyond simple pipeline stages.
