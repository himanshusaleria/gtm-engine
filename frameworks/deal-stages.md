## Preview

The Deal Stages framework maps the natural progression of a sales relationship from first contact through closed deal. It defines six distinct stages — from initial awareness through mutual commitment — with clear criteria for what must be true before advancing to the next stage. This prevents the common mistake of pushing for a close before the prospect has reached genuine alignment.

## What You Can Use Now

- **[Pipeline Scorecard](pipeline-scorecard.md)** — While it doesn't define stages explicitly, the scorecard dimensions (Engagement, Problem Acknowledgment, Timeline, Resources) map closely to stage progression. Rising scores indicate stage advancement.
- **[Discovery Questions](discovery-questions.md)** — Essential for the early stages where you're building understanding and validating the prospect's problem.
