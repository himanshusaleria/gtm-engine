## Preview

The Pain Validation framework distinguishes between identifying existing pain and creating awareness of pain the prospect hasn't articulated yet. It provides a structured approach to quantifying pain across multiple impact dimensions — so you move beyond "they have a problem" to "they have a problem that costs them X and they know it." This is the difference between a prospect who nods politely and one who asks "how soon can we start?"

## What You Can Use Now

- **[Discovery Questions](discovery-questions.md)** — The Mom Test rules help you surface real pain by asking about past behavior and specific costs. Use these to validate whether the pain is real and quantified.
- **[Messaging Structure](messaging-structure.md)** — The Issue and Impact components directly map to pain identification and quantification in your outreach.
- **[Pipeline Scorecard](pipeline-scorecard.md)** — The Problem Acknowledgment dimension tracks whether the prospect has validated the pain in their own words.
