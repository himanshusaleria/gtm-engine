## Preview

The Positioning Levels framework defines three tiers of how prospects perceive your value: as a commodity vendor, as a problem solver, or as an essential painkiller. Understanding which level you're operating at determines your pricing power, competitive vulnerability, and ability to drive urgency. The goal is to always position at the highest level — where you're solving acute, quantifiable pain.
