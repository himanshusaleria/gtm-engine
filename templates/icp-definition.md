## Scoring Criteria

Score each prospect 0 or 1 on each criterion. Sum for total score.

| # | Criterion | Description | Weight |
|---|----------|-------------|--------|
| 1 | Geography | Company in target geography | 1 |
| 2 | Company Size | Within target employee range | 1 |
| 3 | Industry | In target industry/vertical | 1 |
| 4 | Has Problem | Evidence they have the problem you solve | 1 |
| 5 | Tech Fit | Tech stack compatible with your product | 1 |
| 6 | Buying Signal | Active signal: hiring, searching, evaluating | 1 |
| | **Max Score** | | **6** |

---

## Tier Definitions

| Tier | Score | Description | Approach |
|------|-------|-------------|----------|
| **Core ICP** | 5-6 | Perfect fit, prioritize above all else | Personalized outreach, invest time in research |
| **Broader ICP** | 3-4 | Good fit, worth pursuing | Semi-personalized outreach, standard process |
| **Universe** | 1-2 | Marginal fit, low priority | Automated/batch outreach only, or skip |
| **Not ICP** | 0 | Does not fit | Do not pursue |

## Validation Checklist

Before adding a lead to the pipeline, confirm:

- [ ] Matches at least one IMP criterion
- [ ] Scores >= 3 on ICP scoring (Broader or above)
- [ ] Has an identifiable IPP contact
- [ ] Does not trigger any no-go rule
- [ ] Has at least one intent signal (from Signal Playbook)
